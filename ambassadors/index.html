The Tier Ladder

No sales pressure. No donation quotas. Just pure street credit and community impact.

Tier 1: Street Credit Ambassador (0–499 XP)

Unlocks: Unique tracking URL, QR Code, Profile badge.

Activity: Spread digital awareness and attend local ramps.

Tier 2: Outreach Ambassador (500–999 XP)

Unlocks: Printing tools, printable QR cards, flyers, and stickers.

Activity: Leaving materials at boat ramps and tournament bags.

Tier 3: Elite Ambassador (1000–2499 XP)

Unlocks: Regional seasonal windows, species patterns, and shared pop-up tent access.

Activity: Staffing local outreach events and strategy meetups.

Tier 4: Area Leader (2500+ XP + Selection)

Unlocks: Map tiers, full intel tools, and deployment rights for official booth gear.

Activity: Coordinating local teams and official event presence.
