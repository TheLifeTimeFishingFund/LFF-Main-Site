Conservation

Conservation is the backbone of the LifeTime Fishing Fund mission.

Why Conservation Matters

Every fishing license purchased — whether annual, lifetime, or supported through the Fund — directly contributes to habitat restoration, species protection, and long-term access for future generations.

Our Conservation Pillars

  • Habitat Protection — Supporting wetlands, rivers, estuaries, and spawning grounds.
  • Species Management — Ensuring sustainable harvest and protecting vulnerable species.
  • Access Preservation — Keeping fishing available for all ages, abilities, and income levels.
  • Education & Stewardship — Teaching responsible fishing and conservation ethics.

Global Conservation Zones

The Global Access Initiative maintains a growing dataset of conservation zones worldwide, including protected rivers, marine reserves, trout/salmon management areas, and special permit waters.

View Conservation Zones Dataset

How the Fund Supports Conservation

The LifeTime Fishing Fund helps cover the cost of licenses for infants, youth, seniors, disabled veterans, and families in need — ensuring participation in conservation funding even when cost is a barrier.

Every supported license contributes to:

  • State conservation programs
  • Habitat restoration projects
  • Wildlife management
  • Public access maintenance

Join the Mission

Conservation is a shared responsibility. Whether you fish, volunteer, or support the Fund, you help protect the waters and wildlife we all depend on.
