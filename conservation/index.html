Conservation

Conservation is the backbone of long-term outdoor access and the foundation of the LifeTime Fishing Fund mission.

Why Conservation Matters

Every fishing license purchased, whether annual, lifetime, or supported through the Fund, contributes to conservation efforts that help protect fisheries, wildlife, habitats, and public access opportunities for future generations.

The LifeTime Fishing Fund believes access and conservation are closely connected. By helping individuals participate in outdoor recreation, the Fund supports both public access and the conservation systems already operating throughout the United States and its territories.

We believe access creates advocates, and advocates strengthen conservation.

Our Conservation Pillars

  • Habitat Protection — Supporting awareness of wetlands, rivers, estuaries, watersheds, and critical habitats.
  • Species Management — Promoting sustainable harvest, responsible recreation, and healthy fish and wildlife populations.
  • Access Preservation — Helping reduce barriers to outdoor participation while supporting the systems that fund conservation.
  • Education & Stewardship — Encouraging responsible outdoor participation through knowledge, ethics, and conservation awareness.

Conservation Atlas

The LifeTime Fishing Fund is developing a Conservation Atlas documenting conservation programs, wildlife agencies, fisheries management efforts, funding mechanisms, and public access resources throughout the United States and its territories.

The goal is to help individuals better understand how licenses, conservation funding, stewardship programs, and public participation work together to support healthy ecosystems and outdoor opportunities.

Future Atlas resources may include:

  • State Wildlife Agency Information
  • Fishing and Hunting License Programs
  • Habitat Restoration Initiatives
  • Public Access Programs
  • Species Resources
  • Conservation Funding Information
  • Educational Resources

How the Fund Supports Conservation

The LifeTime Fishing Fund helps reduce barriers to participation for youth, families, veterans, seniors, and others who may benefit from outdoor recreation.

By helping individuals obtain licenses and engage in outdoor activities, the Fund contributes to participation in the conservation systems already funded through fishing and hunting programs.

In addition to expanding access, the Fund supports conservation through:

  • Education
  • Stewardship Awareness
  • Transparency
  • Public Information Resources
  • Community Participation
  • Knowledge and Data Development

Join the Mission

Conservation is a shared responsibility. Whether you fish, hunt, volunteer, share knowledge, or support outdoor participation, you contribute to the long-term stewardship of our natural resources.
