The LifeTime Fishing Fund

The LifeTime Fishing Fund is a mission-driven company dedicated to expanding fishing access, supporting conservation, and building a transparent community funding model that strengthens outdoor heritage for future generations.

This repository serves as the public mission hub, transparency center, and documentation archive for all fund activities.

Our Mission

We work to reduce barriers to fishing and outdoor participation while supporting conservation, stewardship, and outdoor education.

We believe access creates advocates, education strengthens stewardship, and conservation helps ensure that future generations can enjoy the same outdoor opportunities we value today.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every person has the opportunity to responsibly participate in fishing, hunting, and outdoor recreation while contributing to the conservation of the natural resources that make those experiences possible.

Beginning in the United States and its territories, we aim to build lasting resources, community partnerships, and funding programs that strengthen outdoor heritage for generations to come.

Why This Exists

Many people who would benefit from outdoor participation face financial, informational, or accessibility barriers.

The LifeTime Fishing Fund exists to help remove those barriers while promoting responsible stewardship of the waters, wildlife, and public resources that support outdoor recreation.
