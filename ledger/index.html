Transparency & Accountability

The Lifetime Fishing Fund raises money to fund fishing and hunting licenses that expand outdoor access and support conservation.

What We Report

As the Fund grows, we intend to publish regular reports that document mission activity and financial performance.

  • Donations received
  • Platform and processing fees
  • Net funds received
  • Current balances
  • Licenses funded
  • Individuals assisted
  • States represented
  • Active roster statistics
  • Conservation participation metrics

Reporting Schedule

Quarterly Reports

Quarterly reports provide a broader review of mission performance, including:

  • Financial summaries
  • Assistance provided
  • Growth metrics
  • Conservation participation
  • Goals and priorities

Annual Reports

Annual reports provide a complete review of the mission, including:

  • Year-end financial summaries
  • Licenses funded
  • Individuals served
  • States represented
  • Conservation impact
  • Strategic objectives for the coming year

Additional mission updates may be published as major milestones occur.

Privacy Commitment

We do not publicly release:

  • Personal addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Information regarding minors
  • Medical information
  • Eligibility documentation

Transparency should never come at the expense of personal privacy.

Long-Term Vision

The Lifetime Fishing Fund is building a permanent access infrastructure.

Our goal is not simply to fund individual licenses, but to create a sustainable system that supports outdoor participation, conservation, and future generations of anglers and hunters.
