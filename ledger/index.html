What We Report As the Fund grows, we intend to publish regular reports that document mission activity and financial performance. These reports may include: Donations received Platform and processing fees Net funds received Current balances Licenses funded Individuals assisted States represented Active roster statistics Conservation participation metrics Reporting Schedule Monthly Reports Monthly reports provide a snapshot of current activity, including: Donations received Current balance New roster entries Licenses funded Mission highlights Quarterly Reports Quarterly reports provide a broader review of mission performance, including: Financial summaries Assistance provided Growth metrics Conservation participation Goals and priorities Annual Reports Annual reports provide a complete review of the mission, including: Year-end financial summaries Licenses funded Individuals served States represented Conservation impact Strategic objectives for the coming year Privacy Commitment We do not publicly release: Personal addresses Phone numbers Email addresses Information regarding minors Medical information Eligibility documentation Transparency should never come at the expense of personal privacy. Long-Term Vision The Lifetime Fishing Fund is building a permanent access infrastructure. Our goal is not simply to fund individual licenses, but to create a sustainable system that supports outdoor participation, conservation, and future generations of anglers and hunters.
