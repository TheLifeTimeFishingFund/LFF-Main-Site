Transparency & Accountability Our Commitment The Lifetime Fishing Fund raises money to fund fishing and hunting licenses that expand outdoor access and support conservation. We believe transparency is earned through clear reporting, responsible stewardship, and honest communication about mission activity and financial results. Mission First The Lifetime Fishing Fund is focused on outdoor access and conservation participation. Examples include: Fishing licenses Hunting licenses Access permits Conservation participation opportunities Other approved mission-related access programs Mission funds are managed separately from commercial activities. What We Report As the Fund grows, we intend to publish regular reports that document mission activity and financial performance. These reports may include: Donations received Platform and processing fees Net funds received Current balances Licenses funded Individuals assisted States represented Active roster statistics Conservation participation metrics Reporting Schedule Monthly Reports Monthly reports provide a snapshot of current activity, including: Donations received Current balance New roster entries Licenses funded Mission highlights Quarterly Reports Quarterly reports provide a broader review of mission performance, including: Financial summaries Assistance provided Growth metrics Conservation participation Goals and priorities Annual Reports Annual reports provide a complete review of the mission, including: Year-end financial summaries Licenses funded Individuals served States represented Conservation impact Strategic objectives for the coming year Privacy Commitment We do not publicly release: Personal addresses Phone numbers Email addresses Information regarding minors Medical information Eligibility documentation Transparency should never come at the expense of personal privacy. Long-Term Vision The Lifetime Fishing Fund is building a permanent access infrastructure. Our goal is not simply to fund individual licenses, but to create a sustainable system that supports outdoor participation, conservation, and future generations of anglers and hunters.
