Access Roster

The Access Roster is the core of the LifeTime Fishing Fund mission.

What the Roster Does

The roster is how individuals request support for fishing access. Every entry helps us understand who needs help, what type of access is required, and how much funding is needed to support the community. Without the roster, there is no mission load, no budget, and no conservation impact.

The roster allows us to:

  • Identify individuals needing fishing licenses or gear
  • Track state-specific access requirements
  • Support infants aging into their first license
  • Assist disabled anglers and veterans
  • Plan conservation participation
  • Calculate mission funding needs
  • Drive ambassador outreach and QR activity

How It Works

  1. You submit the roster intake form
  2. We classify your request (state, age, eligibility, urgency)
  3. We calculate the funding needed
  4. Ambassadors help drive awareness and support
  5. The mission allocates resources based on transparency and need
