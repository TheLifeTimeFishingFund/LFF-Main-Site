Funding Needs

The LifeTime Fishing Fund is a national fund with local fulfillment. This page explains how fishing and hunting license costs create mission load and funding needs.

How Funding Works

Every license we help fund represents access, conservation, and long-term participation in the outdoors. Funding needs are driven by:

  • State-specific fishing and hunting license fees
  • Discounts for youth, seniors, veterans, and disabled hunters/anglers
  • Lifetime vs annual license structures
  • Local fulfillment capacity and ambassador coverage

This page is a simplified public view. Internal calculations, scraping, and fee monitoring are handled by a separate operations system.

Current Fulfillment Focus: North Carolina & South Carolina

While the fund is national in scope, initial fulfillment is focused on North Carolina and South Carolina. Other states are part of the awareness and planning atlas and may unlock for fulfillment as area ambassadors and resources come online.

North Carolina — Fishing & Hunting License Overview

Fishing Licenses (Example Categories)

  • Lifetime Inland Fishing: Higher one-time cost, long-term access.
  • Lifetime Coastal Fishing: Saltwater access for coastal waters.
  • Unified Sportsman (Lifetime): Combined hunting and inland fishing.
  • Youth / Junior Licenses: Reduced fees for younger anglers.
  • Senior Licenses: Discounted or special-rate licenses for seniors.
  • Disabled Veteran Licenses: Reduced or special-rate lifetime options.

Hunting Licenses (Example Categories)

  • Lifetime Hunting: One-time fee for long-term hunting access.
  • Sportsman Packages: Combined hunting and fishing privileges.
  • Youth Hunting Licenses: Reduced fees for youth hunters.
  • Senior Hunting Licenses: Discounted access for seniors.
  • Disabled Veteran Hunting Licenses: Special-rate or reduced-fee options.

Exact fees and eligibility rules are maintained internally and may change over time. Public values shown here are simplified for understanding and planning.

South Carolina — Fishing & Hunting License Overview

Fishing Licenses (Example Categories)

  • Lifetime Freshwater Fishing: One-time fee for freshwater access.
  • Lifetime Saltwater Fishing: Coastal and saltwater access.
  • Combination Licenses: Bundled fishing and hunting privileges.
  • Youth Licenses: Reduced fees for younger anglers.
  • Senior Licenses: Discounted access for seniors.
  • Disabled / Veteran Licenses: Special-rate options where available.

Hunting Licenses (Example Categories)

  • Lifetime Hunting: Long-term hunting access via one-time fee.
  • Big Game / Small Game Packages: Species-specific license groupings.
  • Youth Hunting Licenses: Reduced fees for youth hunters.
  • Senior Hunting Licenses: Discounted access for seniors.
  • Disabled / Veteran Licenses: Special-rate or reduced-fee options.

As with North Carolina, detailed fee tables and eligibility rules are managed internally and may be updated as state regulations change.

Funding Math — “Needed vs Raised” (Concept)

For each roster entry, internal systems calculate:

  • Estimated License Cost: Based on state, type, and discounts.
  • Mission Load: Total cost of all active roster entries.
  • Funds Raised: Total contributions available for fulfillment.
  • Gap: Difference between funding needed and funding raised.

Public pages may show simplified summaries such as:

  • Estimated Funding Needed: $X (across all active requests)
  • Funds Raised: $Y
  • Licenses Funded: N (fishing + hunting)

Detailed calculations, scraping of state fee schedules, and internal ledger logic are handled in a separate operations system and surfaced here only as summaries.
